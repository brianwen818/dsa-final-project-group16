# HW 2: Generic Geometric Progression

**Due Date:** September 24

---

## 📌 General Requirements

- **Inheritance & Generics:** Output two different types of geometric progressions using inheritance and generics.
- **Exception Handling:** Implement `try-catch` blocks to handle exceptions properly.

---

## 📝 Problem Descriptions

### 1. The Growth of Population

- **Input:** 
  - Get the initial population and number of years from user input using the `Scanner` class.
- **Rule:** 
  - The population doubles every 30 years (one generation).
- **Output:** 
  - Print the population progression by generation.
- **Example:**
  - **Input:** `3` people, `120` years
  - **Output:** `3, 6, 12, 24, 48`

---

### 2. The Growth of Capital

- **Input:** 
  - Get the initial capital and number of years from user input.
- **Rule:** 
  - The annual interest rate is $2\%$.
- **Output:** 
  - Print the yearly capital progression.
- **Example:**
  - **Input:** `100`, `2` years
  - **Output:** `100, 102, 104.04`
